# Composite local SH8 receipt replay

This checker will independently replay the complete local C1 and C2 producers,
their fresh verifiers, extracted C2/C3 byte equality, and all 547 original corpus
observations for each extracted compiler. It will read evidence and hash files;
it will execute no compiler, verifier, mutation helper, or network command.

Each generation has two explicitly reused actual controls and nine successful
distinct cases from the failed v2 attempt. Their original paths, commands,
sources, registrations, input identities, error classes, protected outputs and
resource profile remain binding. The enclosing v2 attempt remains failed.

The v3 attempt must contain exactly fourteen fresh cases: the corrected
`rebound-job-limit` case and thirteen unchanged semantic/framing mutations.
Its admitted JOB1 reductions and frames determine the rebound context. Host
ceilings cannot substitute for admitted values. The old and new case sets must
be disjoint and their union must equal all twenty-three required cases.

The final checker will independently replay the v3 shared reservation, original
retained scopes and partials, per-generation registrations, sampled resource
limits, and final successful completion. The one completed C2 mutant can be
absent only through the separately reviewed exact-byte reclamation transition.
Its incorrect-context result will never count as the required semantic case.

The original v2 checker remains byte-identical. Its pinned utility functions and
constants may be imported only after checking its exact SHA-256. Producer and
corpus checks are retained from that reviewed source. New source pins remain
explicitly pending until the root freezes and reviews the runner sources.
Offline mutation tests will target case provenance, admitted context, resource
accounting and source gates. No final acceptance run will occur while actual
case evidence is incomplete.

The final report will separate reused controls, reused rejections and fresh
rejections. This is local evidence replay for the public complete-witness
relation. Release publication, durable transport, succinctness, zero knowledge
and language-semantics preservation remain separate claims.
